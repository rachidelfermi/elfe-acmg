# PP1 source-to-rule matrix

The evaluator is an offline PP1 evidence assessor. Its disease labels are exact scope keys, not inferred diagnoses. All thresholds below are pinned to the stated source/version. Inputs are curated evidence; the evaluator does not validate raw sequencing data or discover relationships. Cases in `tests/test_pp1.py` are synthetic.

| Source and version | Scope and pathway | Minimum sufficient evidence | Permitted strength | Exclusions and unsupported situations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ACMG/AMP Richards et al. 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25741868/) | General conceptual PP1/BS4 guidance | Cosegregation in multiple affected relatives of a definitively disease-associated gene | Supporting; higher strength with increasing data, without numeric cutoffs in this source | A linked causal variant, phenocopies, false relationships and age-dependent penetrance can mislead. This source alone is **not** used for numeric activation. |
| [ClinGen SVI / Hearing Loss VCEP, 2018, Table 4a/b](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6188673/) and [archived specification v1](https://clinicalgenome.org/site/assets/files/2041/clingen_hl_acmg_specifications_v1-1.pdf) | `HEARING_LOSS` in CDH23, COCH, GJB2, KCNQ4, MYO6, MYO7A, SLC26A4, TECTA, USH2A; AD count, classic AR count, or validated LOD | Established relationship, compatible mode and mechanism, reliable variant; AD: 2/4/5 affected segregations excluding proband; AR: validated biallelic trans genotype and classic two carrier parent risk model, with 4:1/16:1/32:1 likelihood thresholds; LOD: validated disease, penetrance, phenotype and pedigree assumptions | Supporting / Moderate / Strong | AR unaffected carrier parents excluded. Unaffected at-risk relatives only count under classic AR model. The archived v1 gene list limits implementation; other or newer Hearing Loss specifications need separate review. |
| [ClinGen RUNX1 Myeloid Malignancy VCEP v3.0.0](https://cspec.genome.network/cspec/ui/svi/doc/GN008?version=3.0.0) | `RUNX1` + `FPD_AML`, AD, affected informative meioses | 3, 5, or 7 validated meioses; proband excluded; phenotype must match the VCEP's RUNX1 phenotype criteria, supplied here as `phenotype_relevant=True` by the curator | Supporting / Moderate / Strong | Only genotype and phenotype positive relatives or qualifying obligate carriers count. Individual obligate-carrier inference is not implemented; supply validated aggregate counts. |
| [ClinGen PTEN VCEP v2.0.0](https://cspec.genome.network/cspec/ui/svi/doc/GN003?version=2.0.0) | `PTEN` + `PHTS`, AD, affected informative meioses | 3 or 4 for Supporting; 5 or 6 for Moderate; at least 7 across at least two independent families for Strong | Supporting / Moderate / Strong | One family with ≥7 meioses is capped at Moderate. Duplicate/related families cannot be combined. |

## Input and decision boundaries

- `affected_segregations` is a **validated count** of informative affected relatives after excluding the proband. It requires `count_validated`, `proband_excluded`, `relationships_confirmed`, `genotype_reliable`, and `phenotype_relevant` to be true. Curated `individuals` can be counted instead; each must explicitly mark an independent informative event. The evaluator never infers an informative meiosis from a family member alone.
- A supplied LOD is usable only when its disease model, penetrance assumptions, pedigree, phenotype model and method are validated. The SVI/Hearing Loss LOD thresholds are 0.6, 1.2 and 1.5. A single valid LOD can satisfy that pathway without individual genotype fields. RUNX1 and PTEN are assessed through their versioned meiosis rules, not those LOD thresholds.
- Known affected noncarriers prevent PP1 unless a phenocopy is established. An unaffected carrier requires age or validated penetrance interpretation. A young unaffected carrier does not automatically count against PP1. Established alternative diagnoses prevent PP1; linked candidate variants and evidence overlap that is prohibited by the applicable specification leave the result undetermined.
- Multiple families can be combined only when each has a unique identifier and explicitly documented independence, no overlap and no duplicated report. Validated LODs combine as independent log likelihood contributions. Different pathways are not added to each other.
- `NOT_MET` means reliable evidence is below the pinned rule or clearly contradicts PP1. `UNDETERMINED` means a potentially applicable pathway lacks needed evidence. `NOT_APPLICABLE` means a known scope or mechanism mismatch.
- PS2/PM6, PM3 and PP4 are outside this engine. `evidence_overlap_prohibited=True` is a curator-supplied conclusion about an applicable restriction; merely listing overlap codes does not suppress PP1. This engine does not assign BS4.
- No universal PP1 threshold, general X-linked rule, or coverage of all VCEPs is claimed. Disease and penetrance facts must be established externally. `KNOWN`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` are accepted in `evidence_states`; conflicting supplied states halt activation.
