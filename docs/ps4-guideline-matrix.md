# PS4 guideline to code matrix

| Path | Source and version | Scope and minimum evidence | Threshold and strength | Exclusions and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Case-control | [Richards et al., ACMG/AMP 2015, PS4 Note 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25741868/) | Matching variant and disease, valid affected and control cohorts, reliable 2×2 carrier counts, adequate ancestry and technical comparability | OR > 5 and 95% CI lower bound > 1: **Strong** | Confounding, uncertain cohort validity, and conflicting evidence block activation. A 2×2 table is evaluated with a conditional exact OR interval and Fisher test. No continuity correction. |
| Published association | Same source | Same cohort validity evidence, reported OR or RR and explicitly labeled 95% CI, source and method | Same Strong rule | Raw counts may be absent. Adjusted estimates retain their method and require adjustment validity. Inconsistent raw and unadjusted reported statistics require review. |
| MYOC proband counts | [ClinGen Glaucoma VCEP MYOC v2.1, PS4](https://actionability.clinicalgenome.org/cspec/ui/svi/doc/635003679?version=2.0.0) | MYOC / open-angle glaucoma; clinically assessed JOAG or POAG probands; PM2 met; unrelated probands from multiple independent studies; exclude probands with multiple MYOC VUS/LP/P variants | ≥2 Supporting, ≥6 Moderate, ≥15 Strong | Individual case, family and study identifiers are required to audit independence. This rule is never transferred to another gene or disorder. PM2 must be curated separately. |
| Multiple studies | [Richards et al. 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25741868/) plus the independence principle in [ClinGen SVI specification overview](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31479589/) | Each association study is evaluated separately | Strong only when a complete individual study meets the base rule | No pooled threshold is specified here. Opposing complete studies return Undetermined for review. |

The 2015 rare-variant observation note does **not** supply a universal numerical case threshold, so the engine does not activate an unscoped case-count rule. It also does not implement VCEP rules beyond MYOC v2.1 or any meta-analysis strength mapping. Inputs are curated evidence, and missing fields remain unknown.
