| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ACMG/AMP 2015, PM4/BP3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4544753/) | In-frame deletion or insertion | Disease-relevant transcript, confirmed preserved reading frame and directional protein length change, nonrepeat region | Moderate | Repetitive region and known benign repeat-length polymorphism; missing repeat status is undetermined. | [ACMG/AMP 2015, PM4/BP3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4544753/) | Stop loss | Disease-relevant transcript, native stop disrupted, predicted or confirmed protein extension | Moderate | Stop retained, absent or rescued extension. No invented minimum extension length. |

Protein coordinates must use the cited reference protein. |
| [ClinGen Rett/Angelman-like Expert Panel, published 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9135956/) | Stop loss in MECP2 or UBE3A | Above stop-loss minimum plus panel profile | Strong | No extrapolation of Strong to other genes. |

The [ClinGen SVI guidance index](https://clinicalgenome.org/working-groups/sequence-variant-interpretation/) was checked. It does not publish a general PM4 strength upgrade or universal indel-size threshold. The SVI working group page now directs readers to consolidated guidance. VCEP specifications change; this package pins one published profile and does not claim current or universal VCEP coverage. Callers must supply reliable annotations, gene/disease context, and relevant-transcript assessment. For multiple transcripts with different consequences, adjudicate before applying PM4. Domain overlap, functional assay results, rarity, and extension size do not independently increase general PM4 strength. Same-consequence PVS1 use is guarded conservatively against double counting.
