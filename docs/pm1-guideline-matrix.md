# PM1 source-to-rule matrix

The evaluator accepts upstream, curated evidence. The synthetic test regions are software fixtures, not published clinical examples. No regional enrichment cutoff is inferred from counts, density, or constraint scores.

| Source and version | Implemented pathway | Minimum evidence | Strength | Exclusions and limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ACMG/AMP Richards et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4544753/) | Verified mutational hotspot | Missense position on matching transcript; bounded curated region; established disease relevance and mechanism; pathogenic clustering; assessed lack of benign variation; credible, independent classifications and source | Moderate | Absent, conflicting, circular, or poor quality evidence prevents activation. No universal numerical hotspot threshold. |
| [ACMG/AMP Richards et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4544753/) | Established critical functional domain | Same location and provenance; clinically critical function, disease mechanism, assessed lack of benign variation, independent classifications | Moderate | Generic InterPro/Pfam annotation alone is insufficient. |
| [TP53 VCEP 2.4](https://cspec.clinicalgenome.org/cspec/ui/svi/doc/GN009) | Codons 175, 245, 248, 249, 273, 282; same amino-acid change in cancerhotspots.org v2 | Li-Fraumeni syndrome; missense; NM_000546.5; position; for somatic route, same-change count and source version | Moderate for codons or ≥10 somatic observations; Supporting for 2–9 | VCEP text still mentions NM_000546.4 within its PM1 subsection while current registry scope names NM_000546.5. Engine follows current registry scope and does not remap coordinates across versions. |
| [DICER1 VCEP 1.4](https://cspec.clinicalgenome.org/cspec/ui/svi/doc/GN024) | Seven metal binding codons; remaining RNase IIIb positions 1682–1846 | DICER1-related tumor predisposition; missense; NM_177438.2; position | Moderate for metal binding codons; Supporting for other RNase IIIb positions | PM1 cannot be combined with PM5 under this specification. |
| [F9 VCEP 2.1](https://cspec.clinicalgenome.org/cspec/ui/svi/doc/GN080) | Catalytic/activation residues; exons 3–5 | Hemophilia B; missense; NM_000133.4; position or exon | Strong for named residues; Moderate for exons 3–5 | PM1 and PM5 combined weight is capped at Strong. Engine requests external coordination if both supplied at Strong PM1. |

The [ClinGen SVI group](https://clinicalgenome.org/working-groups/sequence-variant-interpretation/) and [CSpec registry](https://cspec.clinicalgenome.org/) supply evolving guidance. This offline snapshot supports only the VCEP rules above. Other VCEP genes and published regional statistical calibrations require an explicit versioned implementation before use. Evidence overlap with PS1, PP3, and PS3 is recorded as audit context; these sources do not provide a blanket PM1 exclusion for them.
